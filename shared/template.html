FRANK. Expansion Engine Launch app

Account management that runs on the data FRANK already has.

The Expansion Engine reads every client's usage, finds where they are ready to grow, and takes them from a nudge to a signed upgrade. A person steps in only where the account is big enough to need one.

Launch the app

  2. Nudge one message with their own numbers
  3. Trial one tap starts 14 days
  4. Signed the upgrade goes out for e-signature
